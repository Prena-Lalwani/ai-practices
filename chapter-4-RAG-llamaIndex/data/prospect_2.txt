Company Name: Lumina Health

Industry: Digital Health & Medical Diagnostics

Products/Services: Lumina Health is a B2B SaaS company providing an AI-powered diagnostic platform for ophthalmologists. Their core product, "RetinaScan AI," uses deep learning algorithms to analyze retinal scans (OCT and fundus photos) and assist in the early detection of diseases like diabetic retinopathy, glaucoma, and macular degeneration. The platform integrates seamlessly with existing clinic management systems, providing a second opinion to clinicians and helping prioritize urgent cases.

Target Customers: Private ophthalmology clinics, large hospital networks, and research institutions.

Key Achievements or Milestones: Gained FDA 510(k) clearance for their diabetic retinopathy detection module in Q3 2024. They have successfully onboarded over 50 clinics across the United States, with a 95% user satisfaction rate. A peer-reviewed study published in the Journal of Medical Imaging showed that their platform improved diagnostic accuracy by 15% and reduced analysis time by 50%.

Challenges Faced: The sales cycle for healthcare technology is notoriously long and requires extensive validation and integration testing. Data security and patient privacy (HIPAA compliance) are paramount and require significant investment. The company needs to build trust with a skeptical medical community that is cautious about relying on AI for critical diagnostic decisions.
